## De verificat (neconfirmat sau prudent)
- Louvre RF 1972-8, about 1635 (Wikipedia EN, The Cheat with the Ace of Diamonds); a second version is in the Kimbell Art Museum.
- 'Three temptations' (gambling, wine, lust) is the usual reading, not La Tour's own words.
- That the woman's eyes signal to the cheat is an interpretation.
