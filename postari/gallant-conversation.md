## De verificat (neconfirmat sau prudent)
- Rijksmuseum SK-A-404, about 1654; another version in Berlin (Wikipedia EN, Gallant Conversation).
- Goethe described it as a father admonishing his daughter (Wikipedia EN).
- A coin in the man's hand, overpainted: reported in the literature; not visible, slide says 'experts think'.
- The brothel reading is the modern interpretation, not certain; slide avoids the word.
