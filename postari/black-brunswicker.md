One door. One night before the battle. 🚪

Brussels, June 1815. Napoleon's army is close. At the Duchess of Richmond's ball, officers are called away to fight.

Look closer:
👉 he pulls the door open, she pushes it shut
👉 the silver skull badge of the Black Brunswickers
👉 her little dog looking up at them
👉 a print of Napoleon on the wall

The woman was Kate, Charles Dickens's daughter. The soldier posed separately: the two models never met.

Swipe to see the details 👀

Did he come back? 👇

📍 Lady Lever Art Gallery, Port Sunlight

## Ce s-a verificat (2 nov 2026)
- Wikipedia EN [The Black Brunswicker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Brunswicker): 1860, Lady Lever; el trage ușa, ea o împinge; balul ducesei de Richmond, 15 iunie 1815, înainte de Quatre Bras; câinele; gravura după Napoleon trecând Alpii; Kate Perugini, fiica lui Dickens; soldatul a pozat separat, nu s-au întâlnit; pereche cu A Huguenot.
- **Neverificat / prudent:**
  - Ducele de Brunswick ucis la Quatre Bras pe 16 iunie 1815: istorie generală.
  - Panglica roșie a câinelui și insigna cu craniu se verifică pe imagine.
