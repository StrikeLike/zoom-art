## De verificat (neconfirmat sau prudent)
- Capitol Rotunda version, commissioned 1817, finished 1818, installed 1826 (Wikipedia EN, Declaration of Independence (painting)).
- Shows the presentation of the draft on 28 June 1776; the signers were never all present together: Wikipedia EN.
- Two-dollar bill uses an engraving of it (reverse of the 1976 note).
- Size about 3.7 x 5.5 m: general; slide says 'more than five metres wide'.
