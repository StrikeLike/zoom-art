## Instagram / Facebook: carusel
```
One letter just destroyed this family. ✉️

A comfortable London drawing room, 1858. It looks like an ordinary afternoon. But the husband is holding a letter, and his wife lies collapsed on the carpet.

Look closer:
👉 under his shoe, a tiny portrait of her lover
👉 an apple cut in two, one half pierced by a knife
👉 the daughters' house of cards, built on a Balzac novel, is falling
👉 in the mirror, the door is already open

Egg painted two more scenes: years later he is dead, and she is homeless by the Thames, holding a baby.

Swipe to see the details 👀

Egg never judged her. Would you condemn her, or pity her? 👇

📍 Tate Britain, London
🖼 Augustus Egg, Past and Present No. 1, 1858 (public domain)
```

## Ce s-a verificat (29 oct 2026)
- Wikipedia EN [Past and Present](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Past_and_Present_(paintings)): trei picturi din 1858, la Tate din 1918; nr. 1: soția prăbușită, brățara-șarpe ca niște cătușe; soțul cu scrisoarea, piciorul pe miniatura amantului; mărul tăiat, o jumătate străpunsă de cuțit; castelul din cărți pe un roman de Balzac; oglinda arată ușa deschisă; nr. 2 și 3: anii de după, moartea soțului, soția sub arcadele Adelphi lângă Tamisa cu un copil; artistul lasă privitorul să decidă dacă o condamnă sau o compătimește.
