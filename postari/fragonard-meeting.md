## De verificat (neconfirmat sau prudent)
- Commissioned by Madame du Barry for her pavilion at Louveciennes, 1771; returned to the artist; now Frick Collection (Wikipedia EN, The Progress of Love).
- The reason du Barry returned them is debated (style change to Neoclassicism is the usual guess).
- Fragonard installed them in Grasse around 1790 with extra panels: general history, not re-checked today.
- NO NUDITY: the garden statue has small sculpted figures; no zoom on the statue.
