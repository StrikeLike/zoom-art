## Instagram / Facebook: carusel
```
147 people on a raft. 13 days at sea. Only 15 found alive.

This is not a legend. In 1816 the French frigate Méduse ran aground off the coast of West Africa. There weren't enough lifeboats, so about 150 people were crowded onto a makeshift raft, and the ropes towing them were let go.

When the ship Argus finally found them, only 15 were still alive.

Théodore Géricault spoke with survivors and studied real bodies to paint it. The canvas is almost 5 x 7 metres: you don't look at the raft, you stand on it.

Swipe to zoom in and find the tiny ship on the horizon 👀

Would you have kept hoping? 👇

📍 Musée du Louvre, Paris
🖼 Théodore Géricault, The Raft of the Medusa, 1818-1819 (public domain)
```

## Ce s-a verificat (10 oct 2026)
- **Meduza:** 146 de bărbați și o femeie pe plută, 13 zile, 15 găsiți în viață de Argus pe 17 iulie 1816, câțiva au murit la scurt timp după; pânza are ~491 x 716 cm; Géricault a studiat cadavre la morga spitalului Beaujon și s-a întâlnit cu doi supraviețuitori (Savigny și Corréard). Sursă: [Wikipedia, The Raft of the Medusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Raft_of_the_Medusa). De aceea titlul spune acum „found alive”, nu „survived”.
