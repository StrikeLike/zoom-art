Someone in this crowd was just robbed. Can you find him before the last slide? 🔍

Epsom, 1858, the biggest horse race in England. William Powell Frith packed almost every kind of Victorian into one crowd: an acrobat boy who can't stop staring at a picnic, a con man with three thimbles, and a young man who has just found his pockets empty.

The funniest part? Almost nobody is watching the race.

When it was shown in London, the crowds were so big that a rail had to be built to protect it.

Comment where you found him 👇

📍 Tate Britain, London
🖼 William Powell Frith, The Derby Day, 1856-1858 (public domain)
