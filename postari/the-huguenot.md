She can save his life with one ribbon. 🤍

Paris, 24 August 1572. Catholic mobs are about to hunt down the Protestants, the Huguenots. Behind a garden wall, a Catholic girl meets her Huguenot lover, and she knows what is coming.

Look closer:
👉 a white band on the arm marks a good Catholic tonight
👉 she is tying it on him, begging
👉 while he holds her, he quietly pulls it away
👉 Canterbury bells by the wall, a sign of faith

About 3,000 people died in Paris that day. Millais never tells us his fate.

## Ce s-a verificat (29 oct 2026)
- Wikipedia EN [A Huguenot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Huguenot): 1851-52, Royal Academy 1852 cu ordinul ducelui de Guise („fiecare bun catolic să-și lege o fâșie de pânză albă pe braț”); ea încearcă să-i pună banderola, el o scoate în timp ce o îmbrățișează; masacrul din 24 august 1572, ~3.000 de morți la Paris; zidul pictat după natură lângă Ewell; clopoțeii albaștri pot însemna credință și statornicie; colecția Makins (privată).
