## De verificat (neconfirmat sau prudent)
- Louvre, 1805-07, about 6.2 x 9.8 m (Wikipedia EN, The Coronation of Napoleon).
- Napoleon crowned himself, then Josephine (general history).
- Pope's blessing gesture added at Napoleon's request: widely repeated, not re-checked today.
- Letizia Bonaparte absent but painted in: Wikipedia EN; reason for absence simplified.
- Josephine painted younger: gossip, phrased as such.
